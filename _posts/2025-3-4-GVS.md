# Do I stay or do I go

Now, the question that everyone needs to ask themselves when designing a runic spelling system is: Do I adjust runes to follow the shifts or do I keep the runes in their original place and pretend that people simply changed how words were spelled?

We can call the former the Evolutionist camp and the latter the Originalist camp.

The problem of the Evolutionist camp is primarily that when runes shift, they leave gaps. When Ash moves up to DRESS (think of the word "any"), what goes in the TRAP spot that Ash used to be in?

The problem of the Originalist camp is primarily that you lose a lot of etymological connections between words. In this type of system "divIne" would look quite different from "divInity", "profOUnd" would look different from "profUndity", "serEne" and "serEnity" etc.

# A middle road

I think most people end up going with a mix of Evolutionist and Originalist. They let some runes evolve (like Eihwaz or Ear), while they keep other runes in place (like Ethel).

Unfortunately, I don't think it's possible to be fully in one camp or the other. The pros and cons of each position are too mixed.

One begins to think that there is no point in using runes for English if we also can't "fix" the problems of English spelling at the same time. Part of the "fixing" means returning the letters to their original locations and meanings.

1. Descriptive Spelling

Before the Great Vowel Shift, English spelling was simply descriptive. They changed spellings when pronunciations changed.

2. When checked vowels are left free, they are normally tensed.

The following vowels only exist in checked syllables.

- kit, dress, trap, lot, foot

When these same vowels are left free on the end of a syllable, they become:

- fleece, face, palm, goat, goose

Maintaining these relationships are intuitive to English speakers.

3. Only have a unique rune if a sound cannot be covered by an existing rune.

ᛇ disappeared early on (even before the Anglo Saxon Futhorc existed) because the long sound could be covered by either long ᛖ or long ᚫ. They didn't feel the need to keep using ᛇ just for the sake of "etymology". They happily just switched to whichever best described the sound (ᛖ or ᚫ).
